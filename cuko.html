Cuko

Cuko is a traditional Ethiopian dish made with cornmeal and served with a spicy sauce.

Ingredients

  • Cornmeal
  • Spicy sauce
  • Barely
  • Spiced Clarified Butter (Niter Kibbeh / Dhadhaa)
  • Ethiopian spiced ghee melted down to bind the roasted flour

    • Key Spices & Flavoring

  • Ethiopian Cardamom (Korarima / Aqiima)
  • Black Seed / Nigella Seeds (Tikur Azmud)
  • Finely ground for aroma.
  • Ginger Powder (Ginjaa)
  • Salt: To taste to balance the richness of the butter.

Instructions(steps)

  1. The fine roasted barley flour (basso) is placed in a large mixing bowl or traditional container.
  2. The ground spices and salt are mixed evenly into the dry flour.
  3. Warm, liquid spiced butter (niter kibbeh) is slowly poured in while hand-mixing until the flour forms a thick, dense paste.
  4. It is tightly pressed into a mold or jar to form a fudge-like cake, which can then be sliced or served in small portions.
