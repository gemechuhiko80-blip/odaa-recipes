Doro Wat

Doro Wat is a traditional Ethiopian dish made with tender meat and a spicy sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken or beef, cut into pieces
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups chicken or beef broth
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp flour

Instructions(Steps)

  1. Prep and Marinate the Chicken
  2. Clean the chicken pieces and soak them in water with lemon juice and a pinch of salt for about 10–15 minutes.
  3. Drain and pat dry, then set aside.
  4. Place the finely diced onions in a large, heavy-bottomed pot without oil or butter.
  5. Cook over low-to-medium heat for 20–30 minutes, stirring frequently,
    until the onions softens, lose their moisture, and reduce down into a sweet, caramelised paste.
  6. Add the Butter and Spices
  7. Add the Berbere powder. Sauté on low heat for 10–15 minutes, stirring continuously
    to prevent burning, until the sauce turns a deep reddish-brown color.
  8. Add Aromatics and Simmer the Sauce
  9. Stir in the garlic paste, ginger paste, ground Korarima, and salt.
  10. Cook for another 3–5 minutes until fragrant.
  11. Pour in 1 cup of warm water or stock to create a smooth, thick sauce (kulet).
  12. Cook the Chicken
  13. Add the chicken pieces to the pot, turning them
    to ensure they are fully coated in the sauce.
  14. Add the Hard-Boiled Eggs
  15. Final Touch & Serving
  16. Adjust salt and Berbere to taste.
    Turn off the heat and let the stew rest for 5 minutes.
  17. Serve warm over fresh Injera.
