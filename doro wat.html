doro wat

Doro Wat is a traditional Ethiopian dish made with tender meat and a spicy sauce.

Instructions(steps)

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. Prepare the meat sauce by browning the ground meat and adding the tomato base and herbs.
  4. Prepare the cheese filling by mixing the ricotta cheese with the egg.
  5. Assemble the lasagna by layering the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese filling.
  6. Top with shredded mozzarella cheese.
  7. Bake for 30-40 minutes until golden brown.
